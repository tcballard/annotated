annotated · privacy policy

Keep the moment. Keep control of the data.

This policy explains what Annotated stores when you choose to capture, annotate, publish, or retry a moment.

Effective 2 August 2026 · Version 0.1.0

What Annotated collects

Annotated receives the information needed to deliver the action you request:

  • The source URL, page title, selected passage, and media range you choose to capture.
  • Written commentary or a recorded audio note you choose to publish.
  • Account and session information when you sign in with Google or X.
  • Operational request data such as request IDs, status, and bounded timing needed to protect and operate the service.

The browser extension reads the active page URL, title, and selected text only to show the source you are working with. It does not publish anything until you choose Publish annotation.

Where data is stored

Before publishing, bounded draft metadata remains in the browser. Recorded audio is staged in browser-local IndexedDB until upload or deletion; audio Blobs are not placed in extension key/value storage. Published annotations and media are stored by the configured Annotated backend and its configured PostgreSQL and S3-compatible services.

How data is used

Annotated uses this data to resolve the source, create the public annotation page, deliver media, retry an interrupted capture, authenticate the account, support social interactions, and review rights claims. Annotated does not sell data, use it for advertising, or build a browsing-history profile.

Sharing

When you publish, your annotation, chosen source link, and selected commentary are visible on its public page. Identity-provider information is exchanged only with the provider you choose. Storage and delivery providers receive data only as required to run the configured Annotated deployment.

Retention and deletion

Published annotations and media remain available until the account owner or an authorized moderator removes them through the deployed service. Local drafts can be removed from the extension or capture desk at any time. If the deployed service does not yet expose an account deletion control, send a deletion request through the Annotated issue tracker with the affected account or annotation link; do not include credentials or private audio in the issue.

Security and changes

Requests use the configured service origin, production deployments require explicit storage and identity configuration, and source/media requests apply bounded size, redirect, and private-network checks. This policy may change when the product or deployment boundary changes. The effective date above identifies the version that applies.

Contact

For privacy questions or deletion requests, use the public issue tracker and avoid posting personal data. The publisher is Tom Ballard.
